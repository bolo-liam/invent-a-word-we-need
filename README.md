A make.fun bounty entry: coin one new English word and define it in a single clear line.

## The word

**doorwipe** (n.): the sudden forgetting of why you entered a room the moment you cross its threshold.

English has no single word for the doorway effect — that everyday blank when you walk into a room and your errand vanishes. *Doorwipe* names it: sayable, spellable, memorable.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word and its one-line definition (98 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is needed: the word and its definition are the whole entry.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
